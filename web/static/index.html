Sthira

Your AI forgets what you decided. Sthira doesn’t.

model … open-weight · open source · MIT GitHub ↗
1 · Reads your AI historyChatGPT and Claude exports, Claude Code sessions, meeting transcripts.
2 · Keeps what you settledDecisions with reasons and a verbatim source quote. Nothing is deleted, only superseded.
3 · Speaks upWhen new work contradicts a past decision, it warns and cites where you decided.
4 · Learns from youReject a bad extraction or correct a verdict, and the next run adapts.

Check new work

Paste what an AI agent is about to do, or a plan of your own. Sthira checks it against what you’ve settled.

Bring your own history
